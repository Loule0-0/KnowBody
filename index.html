A Continual Embodiment Harness for Direct VLM Control

A frozen VLM may understand the task without knowing how this robot's commanded motion reaches the functional point that acts on the world. KnowBody makes those body relations explicit, queryable, and revisable.

Zeyu Lou1,2 Yanhong Zeng2 Yong Wang2,3 Chenyang Si1,†

Robot demo

KnowBody in 66 seconds

From task understanding to body-aware action and outcome interpretation.

Method

A harness around a frozen VLM

The body model is not a second policy. It supplies action-relevant relations; the VLM remains responsible for task reasoning and action selection.

What the body model answers

Three relations that change the next action

Real-robot evaluation

75% completion with the same frozen VLM.

Four tasks and four trials per method and task, under fixed reasoning budgets. Cross-episode updates are disabled for this matched comparison.
